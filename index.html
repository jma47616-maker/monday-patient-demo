            teaching pearls
            • "Dark stool but he's on iron" is the classic decoy. Iron stains; melaena is tarry, sticky and unmistakable — the PR exam settles it in thirty seconds.
            • Urea 14.6 with a creatinine of 92: a urea–creatinine rise out of proportion points at an upper GI source — digested blood is a protein meal.
            • The troponin of 34 is a type 2 rise from demand ischaemia on a haemoglobin of 72. Treating it as ACS — antiplatelets, anticoagulation — feeds the bleed.
            • One normal ECG never excludes paroxysmal arrhythmia — but it should not outrank a postural drop, melaena and an epigastric story.
