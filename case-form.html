The Monday Patient
Case submission

Send me a case.

What I need: the clinical bones. Bullet points are fine. Twenty minutes.
What I don't need: code, images, artwork, or anything finished — I build all of that. If a box doesn't apply or you don't know, write "not sure" and move on. A half-filled form is far more useful to me than no form.

The one that really matters is box 5. Everything else I can chase you for.

Nothing is sent anywhere. This page has no server behind it. What you type stays in this browser and is saved as you go, so you can be interrupted and come back. When you're done, tap Copy the whole form and paste it into an email or WhatsApp.

5What else it could plausibly be

The most important box. Two or three competitors, and both halves for each — a decoy only works if it honestly explains part of the picture. "Obviously wrong" options teach nothing: the difficulty should come from temptation, never from hiding things.

6What actually settles it

House rule: no single investigation may hand the diagnosis over. Spread it across all three.

A rash, the stool, the JVP, a blood film. Flag them — those become photographs in the case.

7The traps

What might a competent doctor reasonably do here that would be wrong, or harmful? The best ones are real, from current guidance or a trial — not invented gotchas. Case 1: giving TXA, post-HALT-IT. Case 3: transfusing platelets in TTP.

9Teaching points

Three to five things you want someone to leave knowing.

10Sources

Guidelines, trials or local pathways, with dates. Content has to be current at the time of writing, and I read whatever you cite before building.

11Governance

What makes a case work on here, if it helps

  • The player goes in blind — no specialty, no title, no hints. The presentation has to carry the case on its own.
  • They type what they want to examine and what they want to order. Nothing is listed. Knowing to look is the skill being tested.
  • They commit a diagnosis before they're allowed to treat, and getting it wrong ends the case — so the discriminators have to be genuinely findable.
  • They then write a management plan in free text, which is marked against your bundle from box 8.
  • Roughly fifteen minutes, on a phone, usually at 21:40 on a Sunday.
