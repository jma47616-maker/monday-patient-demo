    Teaching — ABCDE & sepsis
    • ABCDE is sequential for a reason: a problem at A or B kills faster than one at D. Find it, treat it, reassess — then move on. Don't jump to the interesting bit.
    • Hypotension in sepsis is treated with fluids first — a 500 mL crystalloid bolus, reassess, repeat to ~30 mL/kg, and escalate to vasopressors and ICU if it stays low.
    • The Sepsis Six in the first hour: oxygen, blood cultures, IV antibiotics, IV fluids, serial lactate, and urine output. Cultures before antibiotics — but never let that delay the antibiotics.
    • Beware the anchor. “It's just the morphine” cost nothing to say and everything to believe — drowsiness here was hypoperfusion. Naloxone doesn't fix shock.
